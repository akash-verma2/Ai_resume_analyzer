05. Heuristic Quality Index

The 27-Point ATS Rubric Breakdown

25%
Section Completeness

Detects Contact, Summary, Work History, Education & Projects.

30%
Skill Density

Evaluates breadth of frameworks, libraries, and tools.

25%
Action Verbs & Impact

Scans for verbs like "Architected" and quantifiable metrics (% or numbers).

20%
Formatting & Layout

Audits word counts (400–1000), single-column hierarchy, and clean text streams.

Score Formula: Total = (0.25 × Comp) + (0.30 × Skills) + (0.25 × Verbs) + (0.20 × Format) Bounded [0 – 100]

07. Mathematical Formulation

Explainable AI Job Matching Algorithm

Composite Dual-Score Formula
Score = round( 0.70 × Scoverage + 0.30 × Ssemantic )
1. Explicit Skill Coverage (70%)
Scoverage = (|Sresume ∩ Sjob_req| / |Sjob_req|) × 100

Strictly validates non-negotiable required technical skills.

2. TF-IDF Cosine Similarity (30%)
cos(θ) = (vr · vj) / (||vr|| ||vj||) × 100

Evaluates overall domain context and experience descriptions.
